# OVRLand — Final Media Kit

**Operational Vehicle Readout**

**Tagline:** Your vehicle. Your bearings. Your basecamp.

**Domains:** Location • Awareness • Navigation • Discovery  
**Modes:** Drive • Adventure • Camp

## Contents

- `brand/` — full brand guide, palette, JSON tokens and CSS variables
- `logos/` — final dark-background, transparent and monochrome PNG lockups
- `icons/` — app icon plus OVR and compass favicon sets
- `wallpapers/` — 4K, 1080p and Raspberry Pi 1280×800 exports
- `reference/` — earlier OVRLand concept boards retained for artwork reference

## Core palette

- Summit White — `#E8E2D6`
- Expedition Amber — `#F2993A`
- Desert Sand — `#C8B38A`
- Steel Gray — `#6B7077`
- Terrain Black — `#0B0B0C`

The transparent logo exports are derived from the established dark brand artwork and are intended mainly for dark/photographic OVRLand surfaces.
